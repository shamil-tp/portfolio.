RegretMap

Community Map of Travel Lessons

Project Overview

RegretMap is a community map of travel lessons. Travellers share what happened on a trip — the mistakes, the surprises, and what they'd do differently — so the next person doesn't repeat them. Travel smarter, regret less.

Visitors search a destination, explore where stories happened on an interactive map, read and discuss the original experiences, and get a "Before you go" summary and an Ask travellers chat that are grounded only in those stories — every tip links back to its source, and the server rejects anything the stories don't support.

How The Project Works

A Next.js App Router app backed by Supabase (PostgreSQL + Auth). All reads go through a single data module, all AI runs on the server, and Row Level Security guards every table.

1
Search — destination autocomplete shows RegretMap destinations first, then any town worldwide via Photon / OpenStreetMap. Places without stories suggest the nearest destination that has some.
2
Explore the map — every story is a Leaflet pin on OpenStreetMap tiles, with an optional density heatmap and country, category, season and sort filters. The feed loads 10 stories at a time.
3
Destination page — "Before you go" lessons quoted from stories plus a cached Gemini summary where every tip cites the stories it came from.
4
Ask travellers — a chat that answers only from that destination's published stories. Uncited answers are replaced with a fixed "the stories don't cover that yet" reply.
5
Read & react — signed-in users leave regret reactions (👍 😬 🙋 💡) and join Reddit-style threaded discussions up to six levels deep.
6
Share a story — a zod-validated, rate-limited form with place autocomplete or map picking. Stories publish immediately; an AI check only labels them for moderators.

Key Features

  • Destination Search: RegretMap destinations first, then any town worldwide via Photon, with nearest-destination suggestions.
  • Map Explorer: Story pins, density heatmap, country/category/season filters and a paginated feed.
  • Grounded AI Summaries: "Before you go" tips that cite their stories; numbers must appear in the cited stories or the tip is rejected.
  • Ask Travellers Chat: Answers only from published stories; no citation, no answer.
  • Regret Reactions: 👍 Helpful, 😬 Yikes, 🙋 Been there, 💡 Good tip.
  • Threaded Discussions: Reddit-style replies up to six levels deep, with edit, delete and collapse.
  • Story Submissions: Validated form with place autocomplete or map picking; published immediately.
  • AI-Assisted Moderation: AI labels submissions for moderators but never publishes or rejects.
  • Travel Notes: 329 editorial notes covering every Indian state and UT plus 75 world destinations, clearly labelled.
  • PWA: Installable progressive web app.

System Architecture

Secrets — the Supabase secret key and Gemini keys — never leave the server. Clients use a publishable key, and Row Level Security decides what they can read or write.
